Halftone
Interlocked: each spot prints at most two inks side by side. The second ink fills the holes of the first, like tiles, so no ink hides under another and the base cannot show through. It needs tight registration, and its blends can still step in skin tones and sunsets, so Auto does not choose it yet. Choose it by hand and print a test first.
